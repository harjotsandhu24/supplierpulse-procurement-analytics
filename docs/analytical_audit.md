## 3. Duplicate-record sensitivity

The source has 82 rows that exactly repeat an earlier row on all 21 columns ($316,070 in total; 9 rows and $16,385 inside FY2022–FY2026). They sit on 35 purchase orders; the largest single row is $151,200, and 12 of them are amount-only lines.

**Why retained:** the source has no line number, so two identical lines on the same PO (for example the same part ordered twice for two work orders) cannot be distinguished from an export error. They are flagged and excluded from price comparisons, but kept in spend. The test below shows the choice does not change any conclusion.

## 4. Price comparability

- Items: same commodity code, same unit of measure, same full item text, and that text must be on the human-reviewed list of genuine product specifications (`config/reviewed_item_texts.csv`, 153 of 267 candidate texts accepted).
- Period: medians are taken within a single fiscal year; year-over-year changes compare consecutive fiscal years only.
- Rows: unit-priced lines whose quantity × price reconciles to the amount; duplicates excluded; ≥ 4 purchases per item-year.
- Distribution transformers: 20 items had baselines in both FY2022 and FY2023. Median order quantity per line was 2 units in FY2022 and 4 in FY2023. Restricting both years to orders of 1–10 units leaves 16 comparable items with a median change of 34.4% — the increase is not an artefact of order size.
- Within-year dispersion: 153 lines are priced ≥ 1.5× their item-year median ($4.8M modelled excess). If only lines whose quantity is within 0.5×–2× of the item-year median quantity are counted, 82 lines and $2.0M remain, so part of the dispersion may reflect order size. The figure is an opportunity to investigate, not a saving.

### Correction made during this audit

The first version compared any non-blank extended description. Review showed that many such texts were not products (bid/quote references, delivery notes, names, vague text). Restricting comparisons to reviewed product specifications changed the within-year dispersion result from 228 lines / $5.0M to the figures above, and removed one year-over-year action (road-base materials, where the 'items' were delivery notes). Concentration and transformer findings were unchanged.
