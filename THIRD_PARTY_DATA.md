# Third-party data

## City of Austin: Purchase Order Quantity Price detail for Commodity/Goods procurements

- **Publisher:** City of Austin, Texas (Austin Financial Services), via Data.AustinTexas.gov
- **Source:** https://data.austintexas.gov/d/3ebq-e9iz (dataset ID `3ebq-e9iz`)
- **Terms:** [City of Austin Open Data Terms of Use](https://data.austintexas.gov/d/2z87-8uh7). Data "is offered free and without restriction"; attribution is requested, not required. The City does not vouch for analyses built on the data and does not endorse this project.
- **Derived data included:** a minimal-column snapshot, `data/public/austin_po_lines_snapshot.parquet`, built from the CSV the City produced on 5 Oct 2026. Vendor names, addresses, contract names, agreement numbers and all free-text descriptions are removed, and vendor codes are replaced by pseudonymous IDs. Also included: `data/public/nigp_class_labels.csv` (the City's commodity class titles), `config/reviewed_vendor_names.csv` (170 business names) and `config/reviewed_item_texts.csv` (153 cleaned product labels). Columns, transformations and provenance are in `data/public/SNAPSHOT_MANIFEST.json` and [docs/data_sources.md](docs/data_sources.md).
- **Commodity codes:** the City's commodity codes follow the NIGP Commodity Code structure. They are reproduced here only as published in the City's open data.
- **Not included:** the original raw CSV (`data/raw/`, git-ignored).
- **Suggested citation:** Data retrieved from Data.AustinTexas.gov (https://data.austintexas.gov), City of Austin, Texas.

## Synthetic data

`data/public/synthetic/` is generated by this project's code (`src/supplierpulse/synthetic.py`) and is covered by the project licence. It describes fictional suppliers and does not represent any real organisation.
